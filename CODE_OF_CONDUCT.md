# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and productive for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume positive intent; be clear and direct.
- Welcome newcomers and be patient with questions.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, block accounts, or remove contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations will not be tolerated.

This policy is adapted in spirit from widely used open-source community standards such as the Contributor Covenant.
